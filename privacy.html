Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Automation — last updated: November 1, 2026

Who this app is for

This app is a private tool used only by its owner, Bernardo Campos. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app may access the owner's own Google account data, such as Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, Contacts, Docs and Sheets. Access is used only to perform the automations the owner configures, for example sending an invoice by email or reading messages to file personal notes.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed on infrastructure controlled by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored locally on that infrastructure and are not shared. Data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Sharing

No Google user data is transferred to anyone other than the owner, except for emails that the owner's automations send on the owner's behalf to recipients the owner chose.

Retention and revocation

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete locally stored tokens at any time.

Compliance

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
